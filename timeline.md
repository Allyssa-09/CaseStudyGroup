The development of social media, which started with the photo-sharing principles of Instagram and further advanced to the short-form video platforms of TikTok, can be considered one of the most significant changes in contemporary digital enterprise. What started out as the picture-filtering app has evolved into an international business space that influences the entertainment, marketing, and cultural trends. The present case study discusses the evolution of Instagram and TikTok with the help of the main historical milestones and evaluates the change through the prism of the business perspective. Ethical consideration in the future developmental lessons are also discussed.

**Historical Development**

Instagram was launshed in 2010 as a basis mobile application with the focus on the aesthetical sharing of photos. Its portability and convenience won early smart phone audience. The platform expanded quickly and caught the attention of Facebook as an advertising business resource that generates revenue.

TikTok took another course of development. TikTok was created by the owners of ByteDance, the Douyin, which was later combined with the pioneer of musical.ly and its identity was built completely around expressive and short video content (Moou, 2010). The algorithmic model provided new creators with unmatched visibility, which enabled it to conquer international digital culture by the end of the 2010s (Hu, 2024). Although Instagram did not switch to video trends very quickly. TikTok was created to support them, and it radically altered the competitive environment (Muhammad Dimas Prasetva et al., 2025).

**Timeline of Key Events**

2010 - Instagram Launches

Instagram came in as a photo-sharing app, initially mobile, and with one million users within two months it was an indicator of a new digital visual form of communication (Kavindu Millagala, 2022).

2022 - Facebook Acquisition

Instagram was acquired by Facebook at the price of 1 billion USD, as a part of a bigger advertisement and data-driven platform. It was the start of the commercial growth of Instagram as a result of this acquisition (Mou, 2020).

2016 - TikTok Era Begins

ByteDance created Douyin and shortly after getting Musical.ly, they joined the two platforms to create TikTok. This was the beginning of its world wide dominance as a short-form video pioneer (Castillo-Esparcia et al., 2023).

2020 - The Video Revolution

TikTok has already passed two billion downloads, and Instagram has launched Reels in response to TikTok to remain competitive during the pandemic. Video officially turned into the content dominance of social platforms (Ratna Rintaningrum et al., 2025).

***Key Figures and Organisation***

- Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger - Instagram founders that influenced early product philosophy (Hu, 2024).

- Mark Zuckerberg & Meta - Monetised and integrated Instagram (Castillo-Esparcia et al., 2023).

- Zhang Yiming and ByteDance - created the AI-based recommendation engine at TikTok (Mou, 2020).

- Creators, brands, and advertisers - vital to platform development (Kavindu Millagala, 2022).

***Business Viewpoint Analysis***

*Defining the Business Viepoint*

According to the business perspective, technological advancements will be assessed on the basis of revenue potential, competitive advantage, operational efficiency and, long term sustainability. This is in accordance with the analytical perspective of enterprise computing system (Feldkamp, 2021).

*The Transformation Through the Business Eyes*

- Instagram transformed into a marketplace that is fully monetised. The important changes were:

    - Advertisements targeted at users of the Meta information system.

    - A creator economy that is created on sponsorships and partnerships.

    - E-commerce attributes that allow smooth commercial dealings (Ratna Rintaningrum et al., 2025).

The re-named Instagram as a social network and a digital commerce centre.

**TikTok's Disruptive Model**

The algorithm based design by TikTok provided great business benefits:

- Intensive involvement with personalised content.

- Fast creator development because of visibility based on merit.

- High attractiveness to the advertisers of youth markets.

- Its technologically-based model allowed TikTok to compete with long-time established platforms, even when it was new in the market (Mou, 2020).

*Specific Business evidence*

- The adverstising revenues on Instagram increased to tens of billions every year and they demonstrated a profound commericial integration. 

- TikTok was making it to the list of the most popular apps around the world with the help of ads and in-app purchases.

- Both platforms were contributors to the multibillion-dollar creator economy, which serves influencers, small businesses, and digital entrepreneurs (Kavindu Millagala, 2022).

**Connection to Module Concept**

The development of Istagram and TikTok is a goos illustration of the business perspective of the module: it is the technological aspects that transform into strategic assets generating commercial value. Instagram used the cloud-based platform of Meta, whereas TikTok proved the strength of AI-based recommendations. Their development depicts the transformation of compuing systems into enterprise ecosystems.
