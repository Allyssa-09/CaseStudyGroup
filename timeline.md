The development of social media, which started with the photo-sharing principles of Instagram and further advanced to the short-form video platforms of TikTok, can be considered one of the most significant changes in contemporary digital enterprise. What started out as the picture-filtering app has evolved into an international business space that influences the entertainment, marketing, and cultural trends. The present case study discusses the evolution of Instagram and TikTok with the help of the main historical milestones and evaluates the change through the prism of the business perspective. Ethical consideration in the future developmental lessons are also discussed.

**Historical Development**

Instagram was launshed in 2010 as a basis mobile application with the focus on the aesthetical sharing of photos. Its portability and convenience won early smart phone audience. The platform expanded quickly and caught the attention of Facebook as an advertising business resource that generates revenue.

TikTok took another course of development. TikTok was created by the owners of ByteDance, the Douyin, which was later combined with the pioneer of musical.ly and its identity was built completely around expressive and short video content (Moou, 2010). The algorithmic model provided new creators with unmatched visibility, which enabled it to conquer international digital culture by the end of the 2010s (Hu, 2024). Although Instagram did not switch to video trends very quickly. TikTok was created to support them, and it radically altered the competitive environment (Muhammad Dimas Prasetva et al., 2025).

**Timeline of Key Events**

2010 - Instagram Launches

Instagram came in as a photo-sharing app, initially mobile, and with one million users within two months it was an indicator of a new digital visual form of communication (Kavindu Millagala, 2022).

2022 - Facebook Acquisition

Instagram was acquired by Facebook at the price of 1 billion USD, as a part of a bigger advertisement and data-driven platform. It was the start of the commercial growth of Instagram as a result of this acquisition (Mou, 2020).

2016 - TikTok Era Begins

ByteDance created Douyin and shortly after getting Musical.ly, they joined the two platforms to create TikTok. This was the beginning of its world wide dominance as a short-form video pioneer (Castillo-Esparcia et al., 2023).

2020 - The Video Revolution

TikTok has already passed two billion downloads, and Instagram has launched Reels in response to TikTok to remain competitive during the pandemic. Video officially turned into the content dominance of social platforms (Ratna Rintaningrum et al., 2025).

 **Societal Viewpoint Analysis**

Instagram's Social Effects
Instagram has altered how people connect by placing more prominence in pleasing pictures and the ability to build a curated online persona or life. Users commonly utilise their posts statistics to measure the worth of their posts using the likes, followers, shares, and comment statistics as a way of seeking validation. This fuels and alters many things such as social pressure, new norms around beauty, and lying to paint a picture of an ideal life.

**TikTok's Social Effects**

TikTok's algorithm and short-video feed design have been linked to increased likelihood of problematic use and negative well-being outcomes among some users - including higher rates of anxiety, depression or lower self-esteem associated with problematic use (Hendrikse & Limniou, 2024; Brand et al., 2024; UOC, 2025).

Because of this, for certain individials the fast-paced contnent and recommendation dynamics may exacerbate mental-health risks or vulnerabilities.

**Mental Health & Well-Being**

Research on adolescents and young adults links heavy or problematic use of TikTok and broader social-media platforms with higher risk of depression and anxiety (Hendrikse & Limniou, 2024; Brand et al., 2024).

Additionally, among adolescents, social-media use - when intensive - has been associated with lower percived psychological well-being and negative assessments of mental health, especially among girls (UOC, 2025)

**Connection to Module Concept**

The evidence collected from validated studies supports claims that many users - particularly young people - engaging heavily with platforms liek Instagram and TikTok correlates with negative psychosocial outcomes (depression, anxiety, stress) (Hendrikse & Limniou, 2024; Brand et al., 2024; UOC, 2025).

This illustrates how social plaforms may function not just as a means of entertainment or communication - but as a system that can affect mental health, social behaviour, and wellbeing over time.

### **Ethical Issues**

#### **Introduction**
As social media shifts from Instagram photos to TikTok videos, the ethical issues around these platforms have grown. As social media got bigger, companies used more AI and more data to keep people hooked. But that also created problems for users' wellbeing. This section looks at how these changes increased ethical problems and suggest practical ways to make social media more responsible.

#### **Privacy Concerns**
While Instagram used data mainly for advertising, but TikTok's situation is more serious since it operates under Chinese law. China's 2017 National Intelligence Law can force companies to support the government, meaning user data could be at risk of being accessed by a foreign government (Congressional Research Service, 2020).

In 2022, ByteDance employees wrongly accessed TikTok data to track the locations of several journalists, confirming many privacy concerns (Price,2018). After this, the US, UK, EU, and Canada banned TikTok on government devices (Reuters, 2023). Investigations also showed that TikTok can collect large amounts of user data, including biometric details and location information (Forbes,2021).

#### **Mental Health Impact**
Instagram pushed people to aim for perfect posts. TikTok made things more intense with an algorithm built to keep users hooked. Studies show that endless scrolling and personalised videos can trigger dopamine and lead to addictive use (BBC Future, 2023).

Leaked research shows that Instagram makes body image worse for 1 in 3 teen girls (The Verge,2021). The Centre for Countering Digital Hate also found that TikTok's algorithm can start showing harmful content to teens within minutes, including eating disorder videos that have billions of views (CCDH,2022). Studies also show that social media addiction can look like substance addiction (Psychology Today, 2022).

#### **Content Safety **
Instagram struggled with manually checking posts, but TikTok's algorithm caused even bigger moderation problems. The platform automatically promotes content that gets the most engagement, not necessarily what is accurate or safe, which can quickly spread harmful material (The Guardian, 2023).

TikTok's algorithm has been known to push risky challenges and harmful content to teens (The Guardian, 2023). During COVID-19, the app also became a centre for medical misinformation, with a lot of popular videos spreading false claims (The Journal of Medical Internet Research, 2021). Basically, the way the algorithm works can quickly take users from normal content to more extreme or dangerous stuff (Centre for Countering Digital Hate, 2022).

#### **Solutions**
**For Data Privacy**
TikTok should have outside audits of its recommendation algorithm, with the results shared publicly, like the rules in the EU Digital Services Act (European Parliament, 2022).

**For Mental Health**
Make users take breaks after 45 minutes of continuous use and offer a chronological feed to reduce algorithm-driven pressure. Platforms should also make well-being tools and usage stats easy to access and understand (American Psychological Association, 2023).

**For Content Moderation**
Platforms should release quarterly reports showing what their algorithms are boosting, not just what they remove. They should also have independent oversight boards with real authority over content decisions (Tech Policy Press, 2022).


**Conclusion**

The case of Instagram as to TikTok shows how quickly social media could turn into a significant commercial and cultural power. The growth of Instagram into advertising and commerce, along with the TikTok algorithmic innovation, changed the digital behaviour of the world. These platforms demonstrate how economic worth is realised through user data, interaction, and scalable digital infrastructure through the business perspective. Ownership of privacy, wellbeing and fairness are some of the most important elements of ethics that shoul be considered as social media keeps affecting the society. This development underscores the necessity of platforms in the future that combine responsible design and new business approach to secure sustainable and fair digital space.
