### Societal Impacts of TikTok and Instagram

**Introduction**

In what way has TikTok shaped everyday life for us? How does instagram as a platform influence the way people behave with one another? What kinds of social effects do these platfroms inadvertently cause?

Instagram and TikTok have rapidly grown in recent years, this is due to their utilisation of our human nature as social creatures. The ability to share funny short-form videos and easily send someone a message has become a mainstay in how people communicate, form their identities, and take part in digital culture and or communites. What started as fun, creative tools for contacting friends and sharing their everyday with the world now also shapes social norms, peer relationships, indivduals mental health, and how information is tranferred and spread. This section discusses and examines the key moments in the years following Instagram and TikToks release and explores how their popularity has had unforeseen consequences on society as a whole.

**Historical Development**

Instagram launched in 2010 as a simple but effective photo-sharing app that let people apply filters, share images and communicate. Because it leaned so much into the photo aspect, as it caught on fast, especially with younger people. Over time, Instagram evolved into a social space centred on likes, lifesyle presentation, and building a perfect personal image online.

TikTok followed a vert different path. It emerged from ByteDance's Douyin and later merged with Musical.ly so the whole platfrom ended up revolving around quick, expressive videos and attention grabbing thumbnails (Sigian & Yuliana, 2023). TikTok's algorithm pushed its everyday users to create, and that played a big role in shaping the youth culture online at the time (Hu, 2024). Rather than carefully curated visuals, TikTok emphasised fast trends that were designed to be churned out like a factory with a new trend emerging day by day, this transformed how people engaged socially at the time (Feldkamp, 2021).

**Timeline of Key Instagram and TikTok Events**

2010 - Instagram Launches
Instagram launches, marking a shift to online posts with photos/images as the main draw. The site encountered communication between users and the platform would slowly shift toward lifestyle content.

2018 - TikTok's Global Expansion
When TikTok merged with Musical.ly, it became a global phenomenon, driven by viral, trend chasing content. The app was fueled by user interaction, and community (Robert & David, 2024).

2020 - Pandemic Social Media Surge
With global lockdowns, the use of TikTok surged. It became a space for humour, trends, and connection, while Instagram deepened its role in social comparison and personal branding (Ramsden & Talbot, 2024).

**Key Figures**

Zhang Yiming and ByteDance developed TikTok's recommendation engine, a system that has massively influenced how digital culture has evolved over the years and especially when it released (Ramsden & Talbot, 2024). Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger shaped Instagram's early philosophy of clean, easy image sharing (Feldkamp, 2021). Meta's leadership further pushed Instagram into being a social powerhouse of a network with it changing norms around beauty standards with it affecting appearance and identity the most. In the end the biggest social driving force is the everyday users who have cultivated communities, became or supported creators, educator, and activists - who shape trends, share their experiences, and influence how the platforms are overall used in our everyday lives.

**Societal Viewpoint Analysis**

Instagram's Social Effects
Instagram has altered how people connect by placing more prominence in pleasing pictures and the ability to build a curated online persona or life. Users commonly utilise their posts statistics to measure the worth of their posts using the likes, followers, shares, and comment statistics as a way of seeking validation. This fuels and alters many things such as social pressure, new norms around beauty, and lying to paint a picture of an ideal life.

**TikTok's Social Effects**

TikTok's algorithm and short-video feed design have been linked to increased likelihood of problematic use and negative well-being outcomes among some users - including higher rates of anxiety, depression or lower self-esteem associated with problematic use (Hendrikse & Limniou, 2024; Brand et al., 2024; UOC, 2025).

Because of this, for certain individials the fast-paced contnent and recommendation dynamics may exacerbate mental-health risks or vulnerabilities.

**Mental Health & Well-Being**

Research on adolescents and young adults links heavy or problematic use of TikTok and broader social-media platforms with higher risk of depression and anxiety (Hendrikse & Limniou, 2024; Brand et al., 2024).

Additionally, among adolescents, social-media use - when intensive - has been associated with lower percived psychological well-being and negative assessments of mental health, especially among girls (UOC, 2025)

**Connection to Module Concept**

The evidence collected from validated studies supports claims that many users - particularly young people - engaging heavily with platforms liek Instagram and TikTok correlates with negative psychosocial outcomes (depression, anxiety, stress) (Hendrikse & Limniou, 2024; Brand et al., 2024; UOC, 2025).

This illustrates how social plaforms may function not just as a means of entertainment or communication - but as a system that can affect mental health, social behaviour, and wellbeing over time.
